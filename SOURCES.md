## Heroes

### 1 — COP31 climate talks close in on a final deal (Antalya, Türkiye)
- A new package lands after a fight over fossil fuels; delegations have
  until 03:00 GMT to object before a final plenary. Sources: **Reuters,
  BBC**. The summit was scheduled to close on Friday 21 November and ran
  into Saturday (overtime, day 13). Stat strip "~200 nations at the
  COP31 talks" and "03:00 GMT deadline for objections" follow the same
  reporting. No final COP31 text had been approved by the 08:30 UTC
  cutoff — see "Not yet happened" below.

### 2 — Russia fires 400 drones and 110 missiles (Kyiv, Ukraine)
- One of the war's largest overnight attacks; at least seven people are
  killed in Kyiv. Source: **Al Jazeera**. Stat strip "400+ drones fired
  at Ukraine", "110 missiles in the same night", "7+ people killed in
  Kyiv" follow the same reporting. The 400-drone figure counts drones
  fired at Ukraine in the overnight barrage of 21–22 November.

### 3 — Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain (Manama, Bahrain)
- An industrial site burns, no injuries; tankers are hit in Hormuz.
  Sources: **Al Jazeera, CNA**. **Two** US bases were targeted
  (Al-Riffa and Awali Sakhir); two Patriot radars were hit, six drones
  were downed over Riffa, an industrial facility was hit, no casualties
  reported. The "2 bases" figure was verified against IRGC targeting
  reports; a "4 bases" figure circulating elsewhere is not supported.
  Stat strip "2 US bases targeted in Bahrain" follows the same reporting.
